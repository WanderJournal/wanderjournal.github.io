Returns Policy

We want you to love your WanderJournal. If something isn't right, here's how returns and refunds work.

1. Change of mind

You can return your order within 30 days of delivery for a refund, as long as it's:

  • new and unused, with no writing, photos or marks inside
  • in its original condition, including any packaging

Tri-colour and Quad-colour Packs need to be returned as a complete pack.

Return postage is paid by you and isn't refundable. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund a return that doesn't reach us.

Prefer a different colour? Return your journal for a refund and place a new order.

2. Cancelling an order

If your order hasn't been shipped yet, email us and we'll cancel it and refund you in full.

3. Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or isn't what you ordered, please email us as soon as possible with a photo of the problem. You can choose a replacement or a full refund, and we'll cover the cost of any return postage.

4. How to start a return

Email us at wanderjournalglobal@gmail.com or use our contact form with your name and order date before sending anything back. We'll reply with the return address and next steps.

5. Refunds

Once we've received and checked your return, we'll refund the amount you paid (after any discount) to your original payment method within 5 business days. It can take another 5–10 business days for your bank to show it.

6. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights.
